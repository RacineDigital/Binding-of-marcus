## About this game

**Some stories were bound shut for a reason.**

Elias, Marcus's grandfather, made books in his cellar. He is gone now, and the monsters from the
stories he stitched shut are loose among the shelves. Marcus is eleven, lost inside an oversized
hoodie, and the only one left to read them back into the book.

Lost Marcus is a top-down action roguelike in the tradition of room-by-room dungeon crawlers. Every
run is a new book: rooms, curios, shops, secrets and bosses are shuffled each time, and each
chapter is a different corner of the house and of the story.

**Features**

- **Eight chapters and a house full of secrets:** from the coal chute to the chapel, plus alternate
  floors, hidden and super-secret rooms, and a back stair to somewhere Marcus shouldn't be.
- **242 curios** that change how you fight: lasers, swings, sprays, familiars, transformations and
  combinations that stack in ways the book never intended. Every treasure room offers a choice.
- **Ten readers**, each with their own way to play, and a darker mirrored version of every one.
- **Four bindings** to shape a run before it starts: the reader's own kit, or fiery, quick or
  clockwork, each with a cost.
- **26 bosses**, each with its own patterns to learn.
- **Five endings** and Grandfather's 25 notes that tell what really happened in the house.
- **Daily runs**, five challenges, Hard and Endless modes, and 82 achievements.
- **Hand-drawn pixel art** and an original soundtrack for every chapter.
- **Play your way:** full controller support with the right buttons for Xbox, PlayStation,
  Switch Pro and Steam Deck pads, remappable keys and buttons, three save slots, and comfort
  options: reduced flashing, high-contrast enemy shots, HUD text size, screen shake and hit pause.
